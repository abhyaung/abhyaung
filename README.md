<p align="center">
I am an AI-focused software engineer with 4+ years of experience building full-stack products, distributed services, and cloud infrastructure. I work where speech, LLMs, real-time systems, and dependable application engineering meet.

## What I am building

At **[Built By The Trades](https://builtbythetrades.com/)**, I own engineering delivery for **Goose**, a production AI call-intelligence and sales-coaching platform for home-service businesses.

- Ship real-time voice and coaching experiences across **TypeScript, Next.js, Socket.IO, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Anthropic, PostgreSQL, Redis, and BullMQ**.
- Build secure multi-tenant systems with **RBAC, asynchronous workers, native mobile capture, observable failures, and production release gates**.
- Take product ideas from customer problem and architecture through implementation, deployment, validation, and iteration.
- Previously built event-driven financial systems with **Java, Spring Boot, Kafka, SQS, Redis, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, and AWS EKS**.
